My research focuses on developing multimodal machine learning approaches for predicting synergistic cancer drug combinations. My work explores how chemical structure, gene expression, and high-content image-based profiling can be combined to better understand drug response, with a particular focus on whether treatment-induced cellular morphology captures complementary biological information that can support more robust and interpretable predictions of drug synergy.
