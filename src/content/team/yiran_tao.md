My research interests lie at the intersection of neurodevelopment, stem cell biology, and image-based profiling. I use human iPSC-derived neuronal models and Cell Painting to study morphological variation across neuronal identities and developmental states, with the long-term goal of building a reference framework for interpreting morphological phenotypes in human neural models.
